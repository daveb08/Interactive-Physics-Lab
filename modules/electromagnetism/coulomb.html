Key ideas

ALGEBRA
Charge is quantizedq = n e,  e = 1.602 × 10⁻¹⁹ C
Coulomb’s lawF = k |q₁q₂| / r²
Coulomb constantk = 8.988 × 10⁹ N·m²/C²
SuperpositionFnet = F₁₂ + F₁₃ + … (vectors)

Like charges repel and unlike charges attract. The forces between two charges are a Newton’s third law pair: equal in size and opposite in direction, even when one charge is much bigger. With several charges, add the pair forces as vectors, component by component.

The point charge is q = +2 μC. Increase n and the sum approaches the integral. Increase d far beyond L and the rod acts like a point charge at its centre. Close to the rod, the near end dominates and the point-charge estimate is too small.
