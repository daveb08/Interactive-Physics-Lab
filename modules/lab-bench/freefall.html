Find g

Measure the acceleration due to gravity the way you would in a real lab: set up the equipment, take your own readings, record everything, then let the graph tell you g.

Your assignment

Determine g, the acceleration due to gravity, and report it with an uncertainty. Measure at 3 or more heights, with 5 or more drops at each, using one ball and one timer.
