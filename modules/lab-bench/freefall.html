Find g

Measure the acceleration due to gravity the way you would in a real lab: set up the equipment, time the drops yourself, record everything, then let the graph tell you g.

● Practice mode · Earth

Your assignment

Determine g, the acceleration due to gravity, and report it with an uncertainty. Measure at 3 or more heights, with 5 or more drops at each.

  1. Put the steel ball in the release clamp
  2. Stand the measuring stick next to the drop
  3. Pick up the stopwatch
