MODULE 07 / MEASUREMENT AND METHOD

Lab Bench

Real lab work, virtually: choose your equipment, take your own readings, record a notebook, and report a result with an uncertainty.

● 1 lab ready

These labs are about measuring, not about getting a known answer. The physics is exact, but your readings are not: you time the drops yourself and read the scales yourself, just as in a real lab. A good result is a sensible value with a sensible uncertainty, and an explanation of what limited it.
