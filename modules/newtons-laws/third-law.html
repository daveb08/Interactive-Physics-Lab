Reflect on the evidence

Your explanation is not automatically graded or saved.

Ideal massless spring coupling: the interaction is transmitted between the carts with no momentum stored in the spring. Labels “B on A” and “A on B” refer to this effective two-body interaction. A real spring with appreciable mass would require its own force and momentum accounting. Both carts start at rest; the track is frictionless.
