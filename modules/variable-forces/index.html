Show the math

Follows the run while it plays; drag to inspect any time already observed.

Slope of x(t) at t* m/s= v(t*) m/s
Slope of v(t) at t* m/s²= a(t*) m/s²
Area under v(t), 0 → t* m= Δx m
Area under a(t), 0 → t* m/s= Δv m/s

Slopes are measured from the curves by finite differences and areas by the trapezoid rule over the recorded samples; the right-hand column is the exact solution. Tangents appear on the x and v graphs; shaded areas on the v and a graphs.
